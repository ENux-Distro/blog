## What Is extras.emirpasha.com/blog

extras.emirpasha.com/blog is a blog website of Emir YILDIRIM, the creator of ENux to upload his opinions about:

- Programming
- Philosophy
- Everyday Life
- Struggles
- Tutorials on (random) stuff

## From the User's Perspective

extras.emirpasha.com/blog will let's users create an account that requires an email, an username and a password an an ToS (marking it with a [1], we'll get to that later) agreement. 
The user's can view the latest post posted by different people, whether that be the creator of ENux himself, or a different person.

The users can create post, by filling out these required filters:

- Subject: What their post is about, maximum 30 chars
- Title: Self explanatory
- Body: Self explanatory

The users will be able to upload images to the body, and the blog will be able to support writing with markdown and displaying that markdown

You can scroll on posts, like/dislike them (don't have a popping button saying you liked/disliked this post, would you like to comment on it), comment and share
to other people. Basically, Reddit and X vibes
